# Prior work and related submissions

## Mathematical antecedents

- P. Erdős and R. L. Graham, *Old and New Problems and Results in Combinatorial Number Theory*, Monographies de L'Enseignement Mathématique 28, 1980, printed page 50. This is the source discussion of recurring differences and weaker sufficient conditions. [Source](https://mathweb.ucsd.edu/~ronspubs/80_11_number_theory.pdf)
- I. Z. Ruzsa, *On difference sets*, Studia Scientiarum Mathematicarum Hungarica 13 (1978), 319–326. The density is upper Banach density, and the results include finite-intersection and translate-cover conclusions. [Original volume, article pages 319–326](https://real-j.mtak.hu/5479/1/StudScientMath_13.pdf)
- S. Leth, *Some nonstandard methods in combinatorial number theory*, Studia Logica 47 (1988), 265–278, and I. Goldbring and S. Leth, *On supra-SIM sets of natural numbers*, arXiv:1805.05933 (2018). These provide established sparse sufficient classes. The manuscript's separated examples have no recurring fixed three-point translate and hence fall outside supra-SIM by Goldbring–Leth Corollary 4.7. [Leth](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00370556) · [Goldbring–Leth](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.05933)
- Further literature gives recurring prime differences, sparse intersective classes, and realization or packing criteria. The proposed novelty concerns the exact progression hypotheses, the finite circular-gap test and its endpoint, and the centered family mechanism.

## Proposed contribution

The manuscript proves syndeticity from bounded contact between long overlapping progression rows, a quantitative finite-gap criterion with bounded perturbations, and a centered finite-family theorem. It constructs zero-upper-Banach-density inputs and explains the rational limiting obstruction. These are the concrete claims submitted for mathematical comparison and review.
